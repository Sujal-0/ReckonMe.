# 🌟 ReckonMe! Unique Features & Twists

While the core loop of *ReckonMe!* follows the proven success of guessme.io (Answer privately → Guess others' answers), we need to inject our own DNA to make the game go viral and feel incredibly premium.

Here are the locked-in unique features to integrate into the roadmap:

## 1. 🌡️ The "Heat" Progression (Dynamic Categories)
Instead of static questions, the game dynamically shifts tone. Round 1 starts with "Icebreakers" (light, funny), Round 3 moves to "Deep", and Round 5 ends with "Spicy/Revealing". 
- **Why?** It naturally escalates the tension and engagement of the party.

## 2. 🚀 The "Reckon Streak" (Multiplier System)
If a player correctly guesses someone's answer two rounds in a row, they enter a "Streak" (visually shown with flames around their avatar). Their next correct guess awards 1.5x or 2x points.
- **Why?** Adds a comeback mechanic and heightens stakes in the final rounds.

## 3. 📸 Spotify-Wrapped Style Shareables
At the end of the game, instead of just a standard podium, generate a dynamic, animated "Player Card" that summarizes their vibe:
- *"The Open Book"* (Everyone guessed their answers correctly)
- *"The Enigma"* (Nobody could guess their answers)
- *"The Mind Reader"* (They guessed everyone else perfectly)
- **Action:** A one-click "Download to Story" button that exports a beautifully themed image with the ReckonMe logo.

## 4. 🎭 Variety & Bonus Rounds (New Game Modes)
The game will support massive category variety, including:
- **This or That**
- **Never Have I Ever**
- **Most Likely To** (Where the options are the actual Player Names!)
- **Two Truths and a Lie (Player Spotlight)** 

### Two Truths and a Lie Mechanics
If the Host enables this mode, the game introduces a short "Pre-Game Input" phase right after starting:
1. **Creation:** Every player is prompted to type 2 Truths and 1 Lie, and they select which one is the Lie.
2. **The Spotlight Round:** During the game, these custom questions are injected into the mix. When Player A's question appears, Player A does not answer/guess (they just watch). Everyone else is presented with the 3 statements and must guess which one is the lie.
- **Why it works:** It perfectly adapts the standard flow! Player A's "answer" is simply pre-locked as the Lie they selected, so the core logic of "Guess what they chose" remains exactly the same!
